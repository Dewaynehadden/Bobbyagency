1. Terms of Service

These terms apply to all services provided by Bobbyagency ("we", "us"), founded and operated by Timileyin Emilolorun (Timmy) from Nigeria, including website design and development, ecommerce store setup (Shopify, WordPress/WooCommerce, Wix, Webflow), SEO, Google My Business setup, website migration, product research, AI automation and sales funnel design. By paying a deposit or approving a quote, you ("the client") agree to these terms.

Scope of work

Before any work begins, we confirm the scope, deliverables, timeline and total price with you in writing (via WhatsApp or email). Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before it is started.

Timelines & client responsibilities

Timelines start once the deposit is received and we have the content, logins and materials we need. Delays in providing these, or in giving feedback, may extend the delivery date.

Revisions

Each project includes the number of revision rounds stated in your quote. Additional revisions or new features may be charged separately.

Ownership

Once the project is paid in full, you own the final website design and content created for you. Third-party themes, plugins, apps, fonts and stock images remain subject to their own licences. We may show the completed work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Third-party platforms & costs

Platform subscriptions, domain names, hosting, premium themes, apps and plugins are paid by the client unless the quote says otherwise. We are not responsible for outages, policy changes or pricing changes made by these third-party providers.

2. Payments & Deposits

  • A deposit (usually 50% of the agreed price) reserves your project slot and is required before work begins.
  • The remaining balance is due when the work is completed and before the website is launched or handed over.
  • Payments can be made by bank transfer to our Wema Bank account, by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack, or through Upwork and Fiverr for international clients.
  • Prices are quoted in US dollars ($) or Nigerian naira (₦). Payments made in naira are converted at the rate confirmed at the time of payment.
  • Promotional discounts (such as 10% off your final invoice when you pay your deposit early) are applied to the final invoice and cannot be exchanged for cash.

3. Refund Policy

  • Before work starts: if we cannot agree on the scope, price or timeline, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • After work starts: the deposit is non-refundable, because time and resources have been committed to your project. It is credited in full toward your final invoice.
  • Cancellation during the project: if you cancel after work has begun, you pay for the work completed up to that point, and any payment above that amount is refunded.
  • If we cannot deliver: if we are unable to complete the agreed work, you receive a refund for any part of the work not delivered.
  • Completed work: payments for work that has been delivered and approved are not refundable.
  • How refunds are paid: approved refunds are sent back by the original payment method (or by bank transfer) within 7–10 working days. Payment processor fees may be deducted where they cannot be recovered.
  • Projects paid through Upwork or Fiverr follow the refund and dispute rules of those platforms.

To request a refund, contact us using the details below with your name, payment date and amount.

4. Privacy

We only collect the details you give us (such as your name, email, phone number and project information) to reply to you, deliver your project and process payments. Card payments are processed securely by Paystack, and we never see or store your card details. We do not sell or share your information with anyone except where needed to provide our services or required by law. You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time.

Optional automated website audits: when enabled, submitting the separate audit consent form sends your public URL, name and email to the audit service. It submits your public URL to Google PageSpeed Insights for Lighthouse lab measurements and sends a one-time report through our email provider. Reports are held in backend memory for up to 30 minutes, subject to capacity limits and earlier loss on restart; download any evidence you want to keep. Google processes the submitted URL according to its service policies. Scores can vary between runs and do not establish compliance, security or sales outcomes. We do not request passwords, subscribe you to marketing, or store reports in a customer database. Request details are processed in memory; short-lived anti-spam records expire after up to 24 hours or when the service restarts. Email reports remain subject to the email provider’s retention rules. Hosting providers may retain routine request logs. Contact us to request deletion of email correspondence.
