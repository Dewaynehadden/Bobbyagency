Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to take the next step. Still have a question? Let’s talk.

How much does a website cost?

Every project has a different scope. Share your goals and the pages or features you need, and we’ll prepare a tailored proposal with deliverables and pricing before any work begins.

How do I get started with Bobbyagency?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, email your project brief, or use a Request a Quote button. We’ll discuss your business, recommend a suitable approach, and agree on next steps.

Which platforms do you work with?

Our main platforms are Shopify, WordPress with WooCommerce, Wix, and Webflow. We’ll recommend a platform based on your catalog, content, budget, and how you want to manage your website.

Can you improve a store I already have?

Yes. We can discuss a focused refresh, product page improvements, SEO, a new customer journey, or a full redesign. Share your current website and the issues you want to solve.

How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on the number of pages, integrations, content readiness, and feedback. Your proposal will include a realistic delivery plan for the agreed scope.

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive design is part of the process. We check the agreed pages and key interactions across mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes before handover.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We can agree on content updates, troubleshooting, and ongoing website care. The support period and what it includes will be confirmed in your project proposal.

Can I hire you on Upwork or Fiverr?

Absolutely. Our contact section links to the existing Bobbyagency Upwork and Fiverr profiles, so you can discuss and manage the project through your preferred platform.

What is included in a free store review?

You can request a personal review of your public storefront. We’ll discuss usability, product presentation, navigation, and possible improvements. This is a review request, not an instant automated report. Never send passwords or store admin access.

Can you migrate my website to another platform?

We can assess your existing site and plan an appropriate migration. The data, pages, redirects, integrations, and checks included will be specified before work starts.
